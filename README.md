## Business Problem
Wanjiku operates a fresh produce stall in Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing. Using fourteen days of sales records and inventory information, this project analyzes overall sales performance, week-on-week growth, product restocking needs and opportunities for business improvement.

## Top Findings
1. Total sales over the 14-day period were KES 46,450.
2. Week 2 sales increased from KES 22,050 to KES 24,400, representing a growth rate of 10.66%.
3. Weekend average sales were KES 4,900 compared to KES 2,685 on weekdays, meaning weekends generated approximately 82.5% more sales.
